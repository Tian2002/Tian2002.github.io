# 不同主体的时段插画

设计目标：七个时段展示七种不同主体，保留统一的动画背景插画风格。夏日海岸沿用原图；其余为山湖、街巷、森林溪流、麦田、雨巷城市、雪山营地。

生成方式：以下六张最终插画均由内置 image_gen 工具生成，并转换为 WebP（质量 82）。夏日海岸保留原素材。

## dawn

目标路径：`public/themes/dawn.webp`

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: a complete 16:9 landscape background for a full-screen illustrated website, approximately 1920x1080. Create one original polished hand-painted anime environment illustration titled 晨雾山湖. Primary subject and scene: Dawn in a vast alpine mountain valley. A glassy freshwater lake reflects jagged distant mountain peaks, layers of lavender fog drift across the water, a tiny wooden rowboat near reeds in the lower right, and a grassy lakeside path at lower left. Delicate pale peach first sunlight on upper mountain ridges and cool blue mist. No ocean, no islands, no coastal town, no railway. Style: richly detailed animated-film background painting, painterly natural textures, subtle clean linework, cinematic atmospheric depth, sophisticated harmonious color, contemplative and gentle rather than cartoonishly cute. Composition: foreground details mostly along bottom and side edges; keep the central 35% of the image visually calm and of moderate value so white website typography added later remains legible. Strong recognizably distinct environment composition, usable portrait center crop. No text, no typography, no captions, no logos, no watermark, no UI, no border, no closeup characters.
